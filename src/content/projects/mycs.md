## Overview

MyCS brings the services and information of the University of Western Macedonia Department of
Informatics together in a single web application.

## My contribution

Together with my team I built a responsive, centralized university web portal using React and
Chakra UI, streamlining access to the department's separate services through a single, cohesive
frontend architecture.
